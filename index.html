Which loan could fit you?

More options exist than most people think. Tap one to learn the basics.

🏠 FHA loan

Down payment as low as 3.5% with a 580+ score (10% down for 500–579). A popular choice for first-time buyers rebuilding credit.

🎖️ VA loan

For eligible veterans, service members, and surviving spouses: often $0 down and no monthly mortgage insurance.

🌾 USDA loan

$0 down in eligible areas, with income limits. Worth checking if you’re looking outside the busiest markets.

🏦 Conventional loan

As low as 3% down on some programs. Lenders typically look for a 620+ score, and a higher score usually means a better rate.

💵 Down payment assistance

New Jersey offers state assistance programs for eligible buyers. Eligibility and funding vary, so ask me what you may qualify for.

Because the goal isn’t a prettier report, it’s a home. I know what lenders look for, so we focus on what actually moves your approval, not busywork.
